accuracy, **P2** voice/clarity, and **P3** polish. Locations name the rendered
page and the nearest heading; implementation must be made in the source mapped
by `docs/copy-editing-guide.md`, not in generated output.

## Additions that cannot be solved sentence by sentence
6. **Add pleasure to the operative copy.** A short section should ask what
   expands aliveness, desire, rest, play, and collective possibility, while
   stating that pleasure alone does not establish consent or justice.
7. **Add governance for community knowledge and contributor withdrawal.** The
   future opacity archive needs authority, protocol, appeal, and deletion rules,
   not only access-state labels.
8. **Create an evidence-status vocabulary.** Mark claims as argument,
   interpretation, empirical finding, practice guidance, or unresolved question.
   The present voice gives all five the same declarative certainty.
