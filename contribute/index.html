Contribute without disclosing yourself.

The most useful contributions are rarely accounts of perfect relationships. They concern uncertainty, contradiction, breakdown, accountability, and gradual change.

Nothing is published without the terms you choose below, and you can revise or remove your work later. The archive should practise the consent it argues for.

Community agreements

What the commons refuses.

The obvious

Racism. Colonial appropriation. Misogyny. Homophobia. Transphobia. Ableism. Coercion. Harassment. Stalking. Threats. Non-consensual disclosure. Punitive pile-ons.

The subtler patterns

  • — Using radical language to avoid accountability.
  • — Treating polyamory as inherently more evolved than monogamy.
  • — Treating family estrangement as universally liberating.
  • — Treating Indigenous thought as spiritual content available for extraction.
  • — Demanding emotional education from marginalized participants.
  • — Using public confession as a substitute for repair.

⌖ Moderation prioritizes safety, context, proportional response, and survivor autonomy. This project will not promise a perfectly non-carceral community while lacking the capacity to address serious danger.

Governance — in the open

A site about non-domination cannot be governed by invisible authority.

These structures do not exist yet. Naming them before there is a community is the only way they can be built with one, rather than announced to one.

One thing that will need a channel here, once there's a community to route it to: what got said to you, in this site's own vocabulary, to avoid answering something.
