The dispatch

Leave an address, not a self.

An occasional letter when there is something worth sending: the zine when it is printable, an invitation to the reading circle, notice of a workshop, an open call for contributions.

  • — No schedule is promised. Silence is not a failure.
  • — Your address is used for this and nothing else. It is never sold or traded. A transactional email service sends the confirmation and unsubscribe messages and never receives the list itself — full architecture in the colophon.
  • — A pseudonym is as welcome as a name. Both fields are optional, and nothing is validated against a legal record.
  • — One link in every letter removes you completely. No exit survey, no retention offer.

received

Check your email. There is a confirmation link waiting, and nothing is recorded until you click it.

The link expires in 48 hours. If it never arrives, check the spam folder, or simply do nothing — an address that is never confirmed is never stored. Every letter after that carries a one-click unsubscribe.
